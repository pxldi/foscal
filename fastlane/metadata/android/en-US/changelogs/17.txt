Repeating events exported to .ics keep their time across a clock change.

The editor asks before throwing changes away, keeps your draft if Android
closes the app, and moving the start moves the end. Deleting two events in a
row offers Undo for both.

Read-only calendars can no longer be edited, moved or picked. Quick add reads
3:30 as the afternoon on a 12-hour clock. If calendar access was denied,
Foscal now shows how to allow it in settings.
