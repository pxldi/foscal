Foscal now speaks German. It follows the phone's language, or the per-app
language setting on Android 13 and later, and writes dates in the order the
language does.

In the week, 3-day and day views, two events clashing no longer squeeze
every other event that day to half width.

Calendar ticks now follow the phone's own show and sync settings, so a
calendar hidden in another app stays hidden here too.
