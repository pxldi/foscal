0.13.0

* A week grid you can read: an event booked inside another is nested rather
  than squeezed into half a column.
* Drag the grid to draw a new event, or tap it for an hour.
* Guests: see who is on an event, and reply Yes, Maybe or No.
* A join button for a video call linked in an event.
* Give an event a colour of its own.
* Calendar style settings: colours, title size, wrapping.
* Skip short events, or a calendar, in the month grid.
* Snooze a reminder from its notification.
* A new icon.
