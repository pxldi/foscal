Foscal is a calendar for Android built on the phone's own calendar provider. It
works fully offline with local calendars, and online with any CalDAV server —
Nextcloud, ownCloud, Radicale, Baikal — through DAVx5.

Nothing about your calendar leaves the phone. Foscal has no accounts, no
analytics and no servers of its own. The only feature that touches the network
is the optional map picker, which is off until you turn it on.

Views
* Month, Week, 3 Days, Day and Agenda, each remembering where you were
* Keep a calendar out of the month grid while it still shows everywhere else
* Skip short events in the month grid, so timeboxing does not bury the dentist

Events
* Create, edit and delete, including single occurrences of a repeating event
* Repeats, with the original rule preserved for events that came from CalDAV
* Reminders per event, with a default per calendar
* Guests, with a one-tap reply to an invitation
* A join button for a video call linked in the notes or the location
* A colour per event, or the calendar's
* Locations, with offline suggestions from the ones you have used before

Calendars
* Make, rename, recolour and remove calendars kept on this phone
* Import and export .ics files, and open one another app hands over

Reminders that actually arrive
* Exact alarms, re-armed after a reboot and after a time-zone change
* A diagnostics panel that says why a reminder was late, and offers the setting
  that fixes it
